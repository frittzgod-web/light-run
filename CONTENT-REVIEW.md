# Content review: Bible visions (wave 1)

Light Run v10 adds Bible "visions": short runs through worlds from the books of the Bible. A vision opens from a bus stop on the journey (just past Ojota) or from the **📖 Bible Map** on the title screen.

This file lists **every question and every verse** used in the three playable books, so a pastor or youth leader can check them before the teens play. All verses are quoted from the **King James Version (KJV)**. Questions are worded for teenagers. The correct answer is always listed first here; in the game the three answers are shuffled across the three lanes.

**How to send corrections:** mark a line (for example "Genesis Q12: reword…") and send it back. Questions and verses live in `js/books.js` and are easy to change.

**How the content is used**
- Each vision shows **3 answer gates**: one easy, one medium and one hard question from that book. Questions the player has not yet answered right are shown first.
- Each vision has **one memory verse** split into 3–5 scroll pieces. Collecting every piece saves the verse to *My Verses* and to the book's page on the Bible map. New verses are shown first.
- If a player falls in a vision, "Rise again" asks one more question from the same book.
- Stars: ★ finish the vision · ★★ two of the three questions right · ★★★ all three right and the whole verse collected.

**Scenery and bosses (for context):**
- **Genesis: The Garden of Eden.** Boss: *The great flood*. The waters rise! Leap the floating logs and ride out the waves (Genesis 7). When the boss is beaten, the game shows Genesis 9:13: “I do set my bow in the cloud, and it shall be for a token of a covenant between me and the earth.”
- **Exodus: Through the Red Sea.** Boss: *Pharaoh's chariots*. Chariots charge from behind! Leave the warned lane (Exodus 14). When the boss is beaten, the game shows Exodus 14:14: “The LORD shall fight for you, and ye shall hold your peace.”
- **Leviticus: The Holy Road & the Tabernacle.** Boss: *The aqueduct shakes*. Stones are falling! Watch the shadows and keep your lamp burning. When the boss is beaten, the game shows Leviticus 6:13: “The fire shall ever be burning upon the altar; it shall never go out.”

**Other new text:** the angel wings power-up quotes Psalm 91:11 (“He shall give his angels charge over thee”). The shop's new Golden oil lamp quotes Leviticus 24:2 (“pure oil olive beaten for the light, to cause the lamps to burn continually”).
