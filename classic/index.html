Redemption City
Light Run

From Lagos to KM 46 — gather light, help pilgrims, reach the Holy Ghost Service.

How to play

🛣️ Part 1 · Expressway Run

  • Swipe left / right to change lane. Swipe up or tap to jump.
  • Jump over potholes and cones. Dodge danfos, okadas and hawkers.
  • Collect ✨ light orbs to brighten your lantern.
  • At every bus stop, answer a quick Bible question for bonus light.
  • Along the way people will need help. Stopping costs time, but kindness gives lasting light.

⛺ Part 2 · Inside the Camp

  • Tap or drag where you want to walk.
  • Talk to pilgrims with a ❗. Your choices show compassion, love, humility, holiness, selflessness, honesty and forgiveness.
  • Follow the golden arrow. Reach the Main Auditorium for the Holy Ghost Service.

Desktop: arrow keys / WASD, Space to jump.
