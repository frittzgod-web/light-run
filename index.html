How to play

🛣️ Part 1 · Expressway Run

  • One continuous run: Lagos ➜ Redemption City, KM 46. Watch the map strip at the top.
  • Swipe left / right to change lane. Swipe up or tap to jump. Swipe down to slide under barriers, low signs and bridge beams.
  • Run up the ramps onto danfo and trailer roofs. Land on rails to grind for bonus points. Hit an okada ramp for a boost!
  • Boss moments: the Long Bridge storm, the Ibafo go-slow and the Mowe market rush.
  • Jump potholes and cones. Dodge danfos, okadas, trucks and hawkers.
  • Grab ✨ light orbs. At every bus stop a Bible question appears at the top: swipe into the lane with the right answer and run through its glowing gate. No stopping!
  • Collect 📜 verse scrolls to build the chapter's memory verse. Complete it for a big light bonus; it's saved in My Verses.
  • The road gets faster and faster the further you go.
  • Spend 💡 light in the Shop on outfits, lanterns and trails. Do 3 daily challenges and keep your streak.
  • Stop for people in need: it costs time, but gives lasting light.

🧍🏾 Make your pilgrim

  • Tap Creator on the title screen. Pick body, skin tone, face, hair, top, bottom, shoes and extras, or tap 🎲 Randomise.
  • Tap 🎨 for any custom colour. Drag the pilgrim to turn them around. Your name tag shows in camp and on the leaderboard.
  • Outfits and lanterns you buy in the Shop appear in the creator's ✨ Outfits tab.

⛺ Part 2 · Inside the Camp

  • Drag anywhere for the joystick, or tap the ground to walk there.
  • Walk up to pilgrims with a ❗ and tap Talk. Follow the golden beacon.
  • Find the side-quest items. Then enter the Main Auditorium.
  • Optional: visit Sister Funke 📖 at the Prayer Ground for an untimed Bible study. New levels unlock each chapter.

📖 Endless chapters

  • Each Holy Ghost Service unlocks the next chapter with new pilgrims, dilemmas and events. Your virtues and lantern carry over.

⚙️ Settings & pause

  • Tap ⏸ during a run to pause. In Settings choose Graphics (Auto, High, Balanced or Battery saver), sound, music, vibration and reduce motion.
  • Add the game to your home screen to play full screen, even offline.

Desktop: arrows / WASD, Space to jump, E to talk, P or Esc to pause.
