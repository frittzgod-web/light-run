How to play

🛣️ Part 1 · Expressway Run

  • Swipe left / right to change lane. Swipe up or tap to jump.
  • Jump potholes and cones. Dodge danfos, okadas, trucks and hawkers.
  • Grab ✨ light orbs. Answer a Bible question at every bus stop.
  • Stop for people in need: it costs time, but gives lasting light.

⛺ Part 2 · Inside the Camp

  • Drag anywhere for the joystick, or tap the ground to walk there.
  • Walk up to pilgrims with a ❗ and tap Talk. Follow the golden beacon.
  • Find the side-quest items. Then enter the Main Auditorium.

📖 Endless chapters

  • Each Holy Ghost Service unlocks the next chapter with new pilgrims, dilemmas and events. Your virtues and lantern carry over.

Desktop: arrows / WASD, Space to jump, E to talk.
